# ADR-NNNN — Decision title

**Status:** PROPOSED | ACCEPTED | DEFERRED | REJECTED | SUPERSEDED  
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD  
**Decision owner / authority:** name or role  
**Scope:** product area, deployment profile or artifact  
**Related issues:** #…  
**Related decisions:** ADR-…  
**References:** repository studies and external sources

## 1. Context
Describe the problem, why a decision is needed now, and constraints. Separate verified facts from assumptions and hypotheses.

## 2. Decision drivers
List material criteria and their relative importance, or explain why they are not ranked.

## 3. Options considered
For each option, describe meaning, prerequisites, supporting evidence, limitations, unknowns, and operational/privacy/security/interoperability/maintenance consequences. Include defer/do not decide yet when genuine.

## 4. Decision
State one precise decision. If not ACCEPTED, label it proposed or deferred and do not phrase it as adopted policy.

## 5. Rationale
Explain trade-offs and rejected alternatives. Cite repository studies and sources.

## 6. Consequences
Record benefits, negative consequences, accepted risks, obligations, dependencies, compatibility impact, and effects on users/operators/consumers.

## 7. Assumptions, uncertainty, accepted risk
For each item record statement, evidence, confidence, owner, review trigger/expiry, and whether risk is accepted, mitigated, transferred or unresolved. Accepted risk requires explicit authority and scope.

## 8. Verification and reversal
Describe verification method, retained evidence, and rollback/reconsideration triggers. Documentation alone is not verification.

## 9. Approval record
Record explicit approval authority and date. A PR merge is not necessarily approval of every proposal in its diff unless project governance says so.
