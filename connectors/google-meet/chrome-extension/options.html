MACLE — Google Meet local capture

Synthetic test meetings only. Real participant capture is not authorized by the current project decision.

How to use

  1. Open a synthetic Google Meet test meeting in Chrome.
  2. Click the MACLE extension toolbar icon to start recording. The icon badge shows REC.
  3. Click the icon again to stop and save the WebM and JSON manifest.

The capture contains the rendered tab and tab audio output. The microphone is optional and is mixed into the recorded audio only when explicitly enabled and authorized. Under the current project approval, microphone input must be synthetic test audio; do not capture a real person's voice. Separate participant tracks are not available.
